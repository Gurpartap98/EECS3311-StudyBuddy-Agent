Operating Systems — CPU Scheduling (sample notes)

Goals of scheduling: maximize CPU utilization, throughput, and fairness; minimize waiting and response time.

FCFS (First-Come, First-Served): simple queue; convoy effect can hurt short jobs.

SJF (Shortest Job First): optimal average waiting time if run times are known; starvation risk for long jobs.

Round Robin: time quantum; good for interactive systems; too small a quantum increases context-switch overhead.

Priority scheduling: higher-priority processes run first; aging can mitigate starvation.

Multilevel feedback queues: processes move between queues based on behavior (CPU-bound vs I/O-bound).
